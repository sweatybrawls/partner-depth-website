Why it exists

Most affiliate programs do not fail from lack of activity.

They fail because the activity is pointed at the wrong problem. Too many programs optimize for publisher count, vanity clicks, and generic recruitment while ignoring margin, incrementality, partner quality, attribution, and channel fit.

PartnerDepth exists to bring the strategy layer back into the channel. The work starts with the data, then turns into a clear operating plan your team can actually use.

Background

Built from in-house affiliate and performance marketing experience.
